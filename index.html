FAQ

What do I need to send?

A finished stereo mix (beat + vocals together). WAV 24-bit is best. MP3 works. Leave a little headroom if you can, but don't stress it.

How long does it take?

During the preview, a person checks each master before it goes out, so it usually comes back within 24 hours.

Is this mixing?

No. Mastering takes your finished mix and makes it loud, clean and consistent. If the vocal is buried in the mix, mastering won't fully fix that.

Who owns the song?

You do. 100%. We only use your file to make your master and delete it after delivery.

What if I don't like it?

Reply with what's off and we redo it free, as many times as it takes until it's right. Because of that, all sales are final (no refunds).

Will it get me more streams?

No one can promise that. A pro-loud master just means your song won't sound quieter or thinner than the tracks around it in a playlist.
